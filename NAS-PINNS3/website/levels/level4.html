Level 4

Distortion Mechanics

The PINN temperature field from Level 1 feeds a plane-stress mechanical FEM model. Thermal gradients produce non-uniform expansion, causing permanent distortion in the casting. Results are compared against CMM measurements and paper baseline (Fig 17/18).

PINN T-field → FEM mechanics 47 CMM measurement points Paper baseline: 0.75 mm
Problem Setup

Thermal Distortion Pipeline

Pipeline

PINN T(t,x,y) → Temperature field from Level 1 NAS-PINN
Thermal FEM → Plane-stress mechanical solve on casting mesh
CMM comparison → 47-point coordinate comparison vs measurements

Mechanical Equations

σ = E/(1−ν²) · [ε − εᵢₙₜₑₗ]
εᵢₙₜₑₗ = α · ΔT (thermal strain)
∇·σ = 0 (equilibrium)

Takeaways

Key Observations

  • ✓
    Bayesian PINN distortion is in the right order of magnitude |δ̄| = 1.67 mm vs paper FEM 0.75 mm — 2.2× larger, but feasible for engineering use after Level 5 refinement.
  • →
    Thermal accuracy directly controls distortion accuracy NSGA-III has L2=0.513 thermal error (7× worse than Bayesian) and 4.74 mm distortion (6× worse). The mapping is nearly linear.
  • →
    NSGA-II and NSGA-III need thermal refinement first Improving their L2 thermal error (Level 5) should dramatically reduce distortion overshoot — which is confirmed in subsequent levels.
  • ✓
    End-to-end pipeline validated The full chain (NAS → PINN → thermal field → plane-stress FEM → CMM) runs without any manual correction steps.
