Framework levels
7-Level Framework
Each level builds on the previous — from single-shot prediction to multi-domain generalisation.
L1

Single-Shot PINN

Global T(t,x,y) predictor. NAS finds 5×151 relu — L2=0.076, only optimizer hitting target <0.10.

L2

Skip Operator

PINN learns T_prev→T_next. FEM at anchors only. skip=2 actually improves MAE — FEM accumulates numerical error.

L3

Hybrid Routing

PDE residual decides FEM or PINN at each step. Config B: 95% PINN — only 1 FEM call per run.

L4

Distortion

PINN temperature → plane-stress FEM → CMM distortion at 47 points. 1.67 mm vs measured 0.78 mm.

L5

Extended Training

20k epochs + cosine LR. NSGA-II improves 4.5× (0.252→0.055), now meeting target.

L6

Poisson Fine-Tuning

Auxiliary Poisson loss as regulariser. Marginal gains — architecture capacity is the real bottleneck.

L7

Multi-Domain

5 geometries: Square, Circle, Annulus, L-Shape, Flower. Circle L2=0.00014. Flower L2=0.17.
